Ferry

Speak the session. We write the note and the letter, and file your client's claim.

Problem

Out-of-network therapists lose unpaid hours to notes and insurer letters; clients who never get reimbursed stop coming.

Solution

A $50 monthly membership: speak or scan instead of typing, get notes and letters, and every client's claim is filed and chased free.

Goal

50 paying clinicians by Nov 19, each with at least one client claim filed from their notes.

Tech stack

  1. Web app and API: Next.js 16, built. Serves the site, the clinician web app and the client tracker.
  2. Phone app: installable web app at launch (camera, mic); Expo store apps follow on the same /api/v1. Store review time is the risk.
  3. Scanning: phone camera, read on the server, new. Native scanners (VisionKit, ML Kit) arrive with the store apps. Photos stay in memory only.
  4. Speech to text: AWS HealthScribe streaming for recorded sessions, Amazon Transcribe Medical streaming for dictation, decided Sep 27. Audio goes phone → our relay → AWS, inside the AWS BAA. New.
  5. Notes, letters, reading scans: Claude Sonnet 5 on Bedrock, new. The MVP's superbill reader is not reused; letters cite the notes they use.
  6. Data and jobs: Postgres 17 and Drizzle, built. Background jobs and timers (pg-boss) are new. Needs a host with a signed BAA before any real session.
  7. Claims: Stedi 837P, 277CA and 276/277, planned. The member authorizes filing under their NPI at sign-up.
  8. Billing: Stripe subscriptions, new. Replaces the planned per-claim fee, which was never built.

Data

  • Clinician: NPI, Tax ID (sealed, shown as last four), note format and style, subscription.
  • Client: belongs to one clinician; recording and filing consents with timestamps; insurance details from the scan.
  • Session note: text, codes, date. Readable only by its clinician.
  • Letter: draft and sent copy, on the claim timeline the client can see.
  • Claim: the existing 16-state model; client stays payee, Accept Assignment = No.
  • Transcripts and rough notes: sealed under their own key, erased with the key within 24 hours. Approved notes stay.
  • Never stored: session audio, scan photos. Never used for model training: client data. Never in SMS, logs or analytics: health details.
  • No real client data on any service until its BAA is signed.
